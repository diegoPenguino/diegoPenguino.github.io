Legend

Diego was born in Bolivia and raised for some years in Argentina. He studied Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at MIPT, in Moscow, and is now on an AI master's there.

He competed at the IOI in 2015 and 2017. Since 2019 he writes problems for the Bolivian Olympiad in Informatics, and since 2023 he leads Bolivia's team at the IOI, including 2025, when Bolivia hosted it. By day he is an AI engineer and backend developer.
